# Self Served

A native Android app for [Mealie](https://mealie.io), the self-hosted recipe manager. Point it at
your own server and your recipes are on your phone — in the kitchen, in the shop, and offline.

- **Cook Mode:** one step per page, big text, the screen stays awake, and timers picked out of the
  step text ("simmer for 20 minutes") that keep running in the background.
- **Offline:** keep the whole library on your phone; shopping check-offs queue up and sync later.
- **Your network:** a home-network address tried first, extra headers for Cloudflare Access or
  Authelia, and your phone's own trusted certificates for self-signed setups.
- **Everything else:** search and filters, servings scaling with kitchen fractions, meal plans,
  shopping lists, a recipe editor with import from a link, and home-screen widgets.

No account with us. No ads. No tracking. Your server, your data.

## Report a problem

[Open an issue](https://github.com/retr0soft/self-served/issues/new). In the app,
**Settings → Report a problem** opens one with your app, Android and server versions filled in.
Issues are public, so please leave out your server's address and anything else private.

## Privacy

Self Served talks to your recipe server and nothing else. The developer collects no data. Read the
full [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

Self Served is an unofficial app. It requires your own Mealie server and is not affiliated with the
